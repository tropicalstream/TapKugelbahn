trimmed, pitch-shifted and loudness-normalized for the machine. Thank you
to the recordists.

In-game, SoundPool rate control repitches these live: the single xylophone
strike becomes the whole staircase scale, and every clack lands at a
slightly different pitch so the machine never sounds looped.
